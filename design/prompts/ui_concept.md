## `reference/ui_concept_v1.png` (approved direction)

> Create ONE image with the built-in image generation tool. Do not write any code and do not create any other files.
>
> The image is a UI/UX concept board for a mobile party game app: a single wide landscape image (16:9) showing FOUR smartphone screens side by side, left to right, as a storyboard of one evening of play. Each phone is a clean, modern, edge-to-edge portrait screen (no device brand, no notch logos), with a small handwritten-style caption under each phone: "1 Join", "2 Your role", "3 Night", "4 Vote".
>
> The game: a social deduction party game (werewolves hidden among villagers). Friends sit around a real table, each with their own phone; the phones are the narrator. Players join a room, secretly look at their role, close their eyes at night while the narrator calls roles one by one, and vote in the morning on whom to banish. Dead players can watch as ghosts.
>
> Art direction, invent the interface fresh, as an award-winning mobile game UI designer would:
> - World: a puppet theatre by moonlight. A big glowing moon is the hero motif of the whole interface (it can drive the time of day, act as a progress indicator, a timer, or a focal point; your choice, make it meaningful).
> - Characters are flat cardboard puppet cutouts with a pale cardboard rim, shown in real 3D space: tilted, with visible cardboard thickness, casting soft shadows, layered on depth planes (2.5D), like a paper theatre diorama. The two attached images are style references for these puppet figures only (same drawing style, same cardboard rim); do NOT copy their exact poses, design new ones in the same style.
> - The UI chrome itself should feel premium and tactile but restrained, so the illustration shines: think night-blue to ink tones, warm lantern-orange highlights, crisp readable typography, generous spacing, large thumb-friendly controls, clear hierarchy (one primary action per screen).
> - Screen 1 Join: a room code / QR moment and the gathering cast of puppets.
> - Screen 2 Your role: a single puppet turning around to reveal its role costume, private and dramatic.
> - Screen 3 Night: almost dark, eyes-closed state; the moon dominates; minimal UI; it must look the same for every player so nobody gives anything away.
> - Screen 4 Vote: villagers' puppets as choices, a clear vote interaction and a timer.
> - Realistic but legible German UI text is welcome (short words like "Beitreten", "Deine Rolle", "Augen zu", "Abstimmen"); no lorem ipsum.
>
> Original designs only: no resemblance to existing games, card editions, apps or characters. No logos of real brands.

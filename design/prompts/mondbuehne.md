# Prompt: style reference "Mondbühne"

Hero artwork for "Whowolf", a werewolf party game. Wide 16:9 composition.

Look: a puppet-theatre stage made of FLAT 2D CUTOUT elements arranged on clearly separated depth layers (2.5D parallax diorama): far background sky and big moon, then hills, then a small medieval-fantasy village, then characters, then foreground props. Every element is a flat 2D cartoon cutout with bold clean outlines, like early-2000s TV cartoons (generic style, NOT resembling any existing show, franchise or character). Characters: one sly werewolf, one mysterious seer with a lantern, two villagers. Original designs. Strong silhouettes, expressive faces, high production polish, very cool and stylish. No text, no logos, no watermark.

Mood direction: Theatrical mix: a real puppet-theatre frame with red curtains and a spotlight, cardboard props on visible sticks and hinges, mischievous and dramatic, half spooky half funny.

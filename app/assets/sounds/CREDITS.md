# Sound credits

Every sound in this folder comes from a third party and is dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is not required; we give it anyway.
No sound contains speech or words.

Common processing for all files (done with ffmpeg): cut to the listed segment, mixed down to mono,
resampled to 44.1 kHz, short fades in and out, loudness-normalised (EBU R128, about −18 LUFS for
one-shots, −24 LUFS for the ambience beds, true peak at most −1 dBTP), encoded as MP3 (LAME VBR q4),
metadata stripped. Extra changes are listed per file. Freesound sounds were taken from the item's
HQ MP3 preview.

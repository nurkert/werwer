# Surface brief: whowolf.de landing page

Scope: the landing page (`/` German, `/en/` English) of whowolf.de. Mode: **Persuade**.
Audience: game groups who play Werewolf at the table; job: understand the idea in seconds and follow the project on GitHub while it is a concept. Proof on hand: real artwork, real docs, a working pre-release; no players, reviews or numbers may be invented.
Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-1-schachteldeckel.png` (direction "Die Spielschachtel", seed f5e6e6ba, pick).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Whowolf is presented as a premium game you unpack: the first viewport is the box lid on a green games-table felt, not a key-art hero; the page refuses the indie-game default of full-bleed art plus feature rows.

OWN-WORLD: forest-green felt ground, cardboard box with printed night-village lid art and the embossed wordmark, punch-out sheet with role tokens, a navy rule booklet; cream type in a warm book serif, lantern-amber pill as the only call to action, a red rubber stamp for status. Recognizable with all copy removed by the lid, the tokens and the booklet on felt.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the box is a true 3D object (CSS 3D, six textured faces: lid art on top, printed side panels, cardboard underside); scrolling the first screen tilts and turns it with parallax, as if lifted off the table toward the visitor; reduced motion shows it still and flat.

STORY: The visitor sees a real-looking game box, reads "Ein Tisch. Viele Geheimnisse.", understands everyone plays on their own phone and the app narrates, sees honestly that it is in development, and follows the project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: nav on the felt (wordmark left, Spiel · Rollen · Wolfsspur · Docs · Download, DE/EN right). Left ~60%: the box lid, slightly rotated, side flap "5–20 Spieler · jeder mit seinem Handy". Right ~40%: two-line serif headline, three-line supporting copy, amber pill "Projekt verfolgen" with the red "In Entwicklung" stamp beside it; below, the punch-out token sheet and the "Spielregeln" booklet. Footer line "Open Source. Gemeinsam entwickelt."

FORM: "Die Spielschachtel", my top-ranked grounded candidate (rank 1 of 7), chosen by the user as the pick card; seed key f5e6e6ba.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

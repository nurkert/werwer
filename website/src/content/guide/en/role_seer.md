## Her ability

The seer belongs to the **village**. Each night she picks one player and learns their **role**. If the settings say so, she only learns their **side**, that is, whether they are a werewolf or belong to the village. What she saw stays on her phone, and she can look it up again at any time.

In the classic night the narrator calls her after the werewolves.

## How to play her

- **Don't show yourself too early:** if she reveals herself on the first day, the wolves take her the next night.
- **Don't stay silent too long:** knowledge nobody hears does the village no good.
- **Hint rather than tell:** defending or attacking someone pointedly steers the village without outing yourself.
- **Beware of false seers:** a werewolf can claim to be the seer too.

## Who she plays against

The werewolves know there may be a seer and look for her. If the [witch](/en/docs/roles/witch/) saves her at night, the village is lucky. If she dies, the village should think hard about what she said before.

## What you need

- **People:** from 3 it works, from 5 it shines, up to 20 can play.
- **One device per person:** a phone, tablet or laptop. With the app or simply in the browser, on the iPhone too. Everything is in [What you need](/en/docs/what-you-need/).
- **The same Wi-Fi**, ideally. Anyone not on it joins through the server.
- **A table or a circle** where everyone can see each other. Werewolf lives on looking at each other.

You need no accounts, no cards and no game master.

## Before the evening

1. **One person installs the app.** They open the room; everyone else can also play in the browser.
2. **Bring everyone to the same version** if several people have the app. Only equal versions play together, and the app reminds you.
3. **Sound on:** the narrator speaks from the phones. A Bluetooth speaker works too; then one device plays the sound for everyone.

## How the evening goes

1. **Open a room:** the host taps "Open room" and shows the QR code.
2. **Join:** everyone scans the code, types their name and picks a figure. Without a camera, type a code instead.
3. **Set up:** the host picks the level. **Classic** is werewolf as you know it, with closed eyes and the seer, witch and hunter. **Whowolf** adds Whowolf's night with sheep counting and door signs. The **Full stage** adds cupid and the mayor.
4. **Deal the roles:** everyone looks at their phone in secret and confirms.
5. **Night and day** take turns until one side wins. The app narrates; the [rules](/en/docs/werewolf-rules/) explain every phase.
6. **The end:** the app shows who was what, with a chronicle of the game. Then straight into the next round.

## Tips for your first round

- **Start small:** for the first round, Classic is enough. Add the special roles in the second.
- **Phones down by day:** during the day the screen only shows who is still alive. Put the phone down and look at each other.
- **Show nothing:** nobody has to prove their role. Lying is part of the game, but only with words.
- **The dead stay at the table:** they watch as ghosts. Depending on the settings they see more than the living, but must not tell.
- **Latecomers** join by day, as a ghost or as a villager, depending on the settings. The host lets them in.

## Big groups

From about ten people it pays to **lock the room** once everyone is in. Then nobody new can join, while players who are in still come back after a dropped connection. Discussion and vote times grow with the size of the table.

Questions that often come up the first time are in [Questions and answers](/en/docs/faq/).

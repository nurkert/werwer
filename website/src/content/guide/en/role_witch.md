## Her ability

The witch belongs to the **village**. At night she comes after the werewolves and **sees whom the werewolves took**. Then she decides:

- **Healing potion:** she saves tonight's victim. If she is the victim herself, she can save herself too.
- **Poison:** she poisons another player, who then dies as well. She cannot poison herself.
- **Both** in the same night is allowed, and so is **doing nothing**.

Each potion lasts only **once per game**. Once it is used up, she can no longer pick it.

## How to play her

- **Don't waste the healing potion:** who knows whether the first night's victim mattered? Some save it for the seer.
- **Poison is risky:** a poisoned villager helps the wolves. Wait until you are fairly sure.
- **Silence protects:** if the pack knows who the witch is, they take her.

The witch cannot save a victim of the **door signs** in Whowolf's night. If the [hunter](/en/docs/roles/hunter/) dies of her poison, he still gets his shot.

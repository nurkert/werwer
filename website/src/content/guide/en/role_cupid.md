## His ability

Cupid belongs to the **village**. Only in the **first night** is he called, and he picks **two players** who fall in love. He may pick himself too. The two learn who their partner is.

After that, cupid is an ordinary villager.

## The lovers

- **If one dies, the other dies too**, at once and of heartbreak.
- **If both belong to the same side,** they simply keep playing for their side.
- **If they are a mixed couple,** a werewolf and a villager, the two now play only for each other. The wolf in love no longer counts for the pack. They **win if they are the last two** left.

## How to play him

- **A couple across the sides** makes the game most exciting, but cupid does not know who is a werewolf.
- **Picking himself** gives him an ally, but also makes him more vulnerable.
- **Lovers protect each other:** whoever defends their partner too eagerly may give the couple away.

Cupid plays in the **Full stage** level; otherwise the host adds him in the settings.

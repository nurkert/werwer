faq:
  - q: "How many people do you need to play werewolf?"
    a: "Whowolf starts from 3 players, so you can try out how everything works. It gets really exciting from 5, and up to 20 players fit in one room. About one in four is a werewolf."
  - q: "Can you play werewolf with 5 people?"
    a: "Yes. With 5 people one werewolf plays against four others. In the Classic level those include the seer, the witch and the hunter. Rounds are short and good for starting out."
  - q: "Can you play werewolf without a narrator?"
    a: "Yes, that is exactly what Whowolf is for. The app is the narrator: it deals the roles, calls the night, counts the votes and keeps to the rules. So everyone plays, and nobody has to be the game master."
  - q: "Is Whowolf free?"
    a: "Yes. Whowolf is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. It is open source under the Apache License 2.0."
  - q: "Do I need an account?"
    a: "No. You only type a name for the round. There is no sign-up, no account and no tracking."
  - q: "Does everyone need the app?"
    a: "No. One person needs the app to open the room. Everyone else can also play in the browser by scanning the QR code."
  - q: "Does Whowolf work on the iPhone?"
    a: "Yes, in the browser with Safari, without installing anything. If you want the app, install it through AltStore or SideStore. Whowolf is not on the App Store yet."
  - q: "Can you play werewolf online?"
    a: "Whowolf is made for people sitting together. Anyone not on the same Wi-Fi can join through our server, for example on mobile data. It is not meant for groups who cannot see each other: werewolf lives on looking at each other."
  - q: "What happens to our data?"
    a: "The game runs on the host’s phone and stays at the table. Every connection is end-to-end encrypted, also through the server, which only passes on what it cannot read. More in the Safety chapter."
  - q: "Can someone join later?"
    a: "Yes, by day. The host lets the newcomer in, as a ghost or as a villager depending on the settings. Once the room is locked, nobody new can join."
  - q: "Which roles are there?"
    a: "Villager, werewolf, seer, witch, hunter and cupid. On top of that the village can elect a mayor whose vote counts twice."
  - q: "How long does a round take?"
    a: "That depends on the number of players and the times you set. Every phase has a clock so the game keeps moving. Small rounds are over sooner, big ones take longer."

## Their task

Most people at the table are villagers. They belong to the **village** and win when no werewolf is left. At night the villager has nothing to do; by day all the more: listen, watch, talk and vote well.

## How to play them

- **Pay attention:** who talks a lot, who not at all? Who changes their mind as soon as it gets tight?
- **Remember the votes:** who voted against whom? Werewolves often protect each other.
- **Don't decide too early:** a rushed verdict often hits an innocent, and that pleases the wolves.

## At night

In the **classic night** the villager keeps their eyes closed. In **Whowolf's night** they tap anyway: they count [sheep](/en/docs/how-to-play/) or make a neutral pick. So nobody at the table can see who is choosing a victim. In the morning the app shows how many sheep everyone counted, a hint but no proof.

The quiet villagers are a good hiding place for the werewolves, and a puzzle for the village.

## His ability

The hunter belongs to the **village**. While he lives, he is an ordinary villager. But when he dies, however it happens, he has a **last shot**:

- killed by the **werewolves** at night,
- **voted out** by the village,
- **poisoned** by the witch,
- or of **heartbreak**, because his partner from the lovers died.

The app then gives him **30 seconds** to point at a player. Whoever he hits dies too. If he lets the time run out, he does not shoot.

## How to play him

- **Listen well:** the shot comes suddenly. Whoever paid attention hits the right one.
- **Show yourself when it fits:** a known hunter deters the wolves, because taking him may cost them one of their own.
- **Don't shoot out of revenge:** whoever voted him out is not automatically a werewolf.

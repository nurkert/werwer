## A round in one minute

1. **Someone opens a room.** One phone taps “Open a room” and becomes the host. It plays along like everyone else.
2. **Everyone else joins.** They tap “Join a room” and then the room that shows up there, or scan the QR code on the host’s screen.
3. **Everyone picks a figure.** It is only how you look at the table and says nothing about your role.
4. **Roles are dealt in secret.** Everyone looks at their own phone alone: village or werewolf?
5. **At night, everyone taps.** The werewolves pick a victim, the seer looks into someone, everyone else counts sheep. From the outside, it all looks the same.
6. **By day you talk, then you vote.** You argue at the table, the app counts. This goes on until the village has found every werewolf or the werewolves have taken over.

The full walkthrough is in [How to play](/en/docs/how-to-play/).

## Their task

The **werewolves** are the other side. They win when they are **at least as many as everyone else still alive**. About one player in four is a werewolf, at least one. The werewolves know each other: when the roles are revealed, every wolf sees who else is in the pack.

## At night

The werewolves pick a victim together. Each wolf taps a player on their phone, and the pack sees whom the others picked. In the end the pack's majority counts. If it is a tie, nobody dies that night, so better agree.

In **Whowolf's night** the hunt always lasts the same time, however fast the pack agrees. So even a quick pack gives nothing away. With **door signs**, the pack may also enter one spotted sign per night. If it is right, that person also dies with a 50 percent chance.

## By day

By day the werewolf is an ordinary villager, or at least pretends to be:

- **Join in, don't hide:** whoever stays silent looks suspicious.
- **Steer suspicion,** but not too obviously.
- **Sacrifice a packmate if you must:** voting against a wolf makes you look innocent.

## The most dangerous opponents

The [seer](/en/docs/roles/seer/) can unmask a wolf, the [witch](/en/docs/roles/witch/) can save the victim, and the [hunter](/en/docs/roles/hunter/) can take a wolf with him when he falls. A wolf whom [cupid](/en/docs/roles/cupid/) makes fall in love with a villager no longer plays for the pack, but with their partner.

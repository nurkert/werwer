## An office, not a role

The mayor is a **setting**: the host turns it on, and it is part of the **Full stage** level. Anyone can become mayor, whatever their secret role. Even a werewolf.

## The election

When there is no living mayor, the village elects one by day, **openly, as if by a show of hands**:

- Every living player taps their candidate in a dedicated election menu and confirms. You may pick yourself too.
- Everyone sees **who backs whom**. Changing your pick is allowed until it is decided.
- **It is decided** once everyone has voted and one player leads alone, at the latest when the discussion ends.
- On a **tie**, the village stays without a mayor until the next day.

The winner gets the **chain of office**: it appears on their card on every phone, and they briefly stand tall for everyone.

## The double vote

In the vote, the mayor's vote counts **twice**. That makes them powerful, and a target for the werewolves. **If they die,** the village elects a new one the next day.

## Tips

- **Choose carefully:** a werewolf with a double vote is dangerous for the village.
- **Watch who runs:** whoever campaigns hard for the office may have a reason.
